The side plank

It builds the muscles down the side of your trunk that stop you folding sideways.

Filming soonThe instructions are all here in the meantime.

Film on its way

Set up

  • Lie on your side with your knees bent
  • Propped up on your bottom forearm, elbow directly under your shoulder
  • Set your neutral spine and brace your middle before you lift anything

The movement

Push down through your forearm and your bottom knee, and lift your hips up off the floor until your body makes one straight line from your knees, through your hips, to your shoulders. Hold that line, steady and still, for as long as you can keep good form, then lower under control. Rest, and repeat, then turn over and do the other side.

What to get right

  • One long, straight line from knees to head. Picture a plank, and keep it a plank
  • Hips lifted and level, not sagging down towards the floor and not hitched up too high
  • The top shoulder not rolling forward and the hips not drifting back. Stay square, as if you were pressed flat between two panes of glass
  • The brace firm, and keep breathing behind it

How much

  • Build towards a 20 to 30 second hold each side, a couple of rounds
  • Expect one side to be weaker, so give it the extra

Easier

  • The bird-dog. On your hands and knees, neutral spine set and braced, reach one arm forward and the opposite leg back until both are straight and level with your body, then bring them back and swap the other way. Same three-three-three tempo as the dead bug. No sagging in the middle, no tipping to one side

Harder

  • A full side plank from your feet, legs straight and stacked, once the bent-knee version feels solid. A big step up in load

Why your back should not be bending
