Reading yourself · Chapter 6

The hinge

It shows you whether your back holds its shape when you bend.

Filming soonThe instructions are all here in the meantime.

Film on its way

You'll needA mirror to your side, or your phone propped up filming you side on. It's the only angle that shows what the back is really doing

Set up

  • Stand tall, feet about hip-width apart
  • Soften your knees, just a little bend, and keep them there

The movement

Push your hips back behind you, as if you were closing a car door with your backside, and let your chest tip forward as they go. It's a tip, not a sit. Your hips go back, not down. Keep your knees bent no more than they were at the start, that same soft bend the whole way, and don't let them travel forward. Let your arms hang loose. Don't reach for the floor, reaching only makes you round your shoulders to get there. Just tip as far as feels comfortable, then come back up to tall. Do it slowly, a few times, so the mirror or the camera catches it.

What to watch

  • The line of your back as you tip. Does it stay long and straight, one flat line from hips to head, like a tabletop?
  • Or does it curl over, the lower back rounding as you go down?

What your reading is telling you

A test tells you what is happening. It does not tell you why, or what to do about it. That is the chapter this one comes from.
