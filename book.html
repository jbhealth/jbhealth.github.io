Try one now, free

Here's one of the tests from the book. It reads your hips, and the hip is the quiet driver behind more aches than any joint in the body, handing its trouble down to the knee or up to the back and letting them do the complaining. Takes a minute, and all you need is a chair.

The figure-four

Sit tall on a firm chair and cross one ankle up onto the opposite knee, so your legs make the shape of a number four. Let the raised knee relax open and drop towards the floor. Then, keeping your back long and straight, lean your chest forward from the hips, slowly, until you feel the stretch build. No bouncing, no forcing. Swap sides and compare.

A hip with room to move lets that knee relax down until it sits almost level with the other, and lets you lean your chest a good way forward over it. Notice how far the knee drops, where the pull lands, and the difference between your two sides, because one will usually have more to say than the other.
