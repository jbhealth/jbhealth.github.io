One test from the book

It reads your hips, and the hip is the driver behind more aches than any joint in the body, handing its trouble down to the knee or up to the back and letting them do the complaining. Takes a minute, and all you need is a chair.

The figure-four

Sit tall on a firm chair and cross one ankle up onto the opposite knee, so your legs make the shape of a number four. Let the raised knee relax open and drop towards the floor. Then, keeping your back long and straight, lean your chest forward from the hips, slowly, until you feel the stretch build. No bouncing, no forcing. Swap sides and compare.

Watch the film of this test →

If a knee stayed stuck up high, or the lean was blocked almost before it started, that hip has run out of room. That’s exactly the kind of thing the book teaches you to spot, and put right.
