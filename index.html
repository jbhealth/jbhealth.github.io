Make a Fuss

A book for anyone over fifty who’s been told their aches are just their age, and would rather not take that lying down. You’ll learn to read your own body with a few simple tests, trace each ache back to the fault that’s actually driving it, and fix it at the root. Then six straightforward moves and two short sessions a week build the strength that keeps you out of trouble for good. No jargon. No gym required. And every test and exercise in it has a short film to go with it.

Try one now, free

This one reads your hips, and the hip is the quiet driver behind more aches than any joint in the body. All you need is a chair.

Sit tall on a firm chair and cross one ankle up onto the opposite knee, so your legs make the shape of a number four. Let the raised knee relax open and drop towards the floor. Then, keeping your back long, lean your chest forward from the hips, slowly, until you feel the stretch build. No bouncing, no forcing. Swap sides.

Notice how far the knee drops, where the pull lands, and how your two sides compare, because one will usually have more to say than the other. Nearly everyone feels something, at the front of the hip or deep in the back of it. What your reading means, and how to fix it, is Chapter 10.
